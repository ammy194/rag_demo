Cricket is one of the most popular sports in the world, especially in countries like India, Australia, England, 

Pakistan, and New Zealand. It is played between two teams of eleven players each, with the main objective being 

to score more runs than the opponent. The sport is played in three main formats: Test cricket, which can last up to

 five days and is considered the purest form of the game; One Day Internationals (ODIs), which are limited to 50 overs per 

 side; and T20 cricket, the shortest and most explosive format with 20 overs per side. Over the years, cricket has produced 

 legendary players, including **Sachin Tendulkar**, who is widely regarded as one of the greatest batsmen of all time and holds

  the record for 100 international centuries. In the modern era, **Virat Kohli** is known for his consistency and ability 

  to chase targets under pressure, while players like **Babar Azam**, **Kane Williamson**, and **Ben Stokes** are admired 

  for their skill and leadership. In bowling, **Muttiah Muralitharan** holds the record for the most wickets in international 

  cricket, showcasing the importance of bowlers alongside batsmen. Major tournaments like the ICC Cricket World Cup and

   leagues such as the Indian Premier League (IPL) attract millions of fans worldwide. Cricket is not just about physical 

   ability; it also requires strategy, patience, teamwork, and strong mental focus, which is why it continues to be one of

    the most loved and competitive sports globally.Cricket has evolved significantly with technology such as DRS (Decision Review System),
    
     which helps umpires make more accurate decisions. The sport is governed internationally by the International Cricket Council,

     
      commonly known as the ICC. Historic rivalries like India vs Pakistan and England vs Australia (The Ashes) create intense
     
       excitement among fans. Fielding has become increasingly important in modern cricket, with athletic catches and quick 
     
       run-outs often changing the outcome of matches. All-rounders, who can both bat and bowl effectively, play a crucial role
     
        in balancing a team. Women’s cricket has also grown tremendously, with players like Meg Lanning and Smriti Mandhana gaining
     
         global recognition. T20 leagues around the world have made cricket faster and more entertaining for younger audiences.
     
     
          Fitness and power-hitting have become major aspects of the modern game. Captains play a vital role in setting field
     
           placements and making bowling changes strategically. Overall, cricket continues to grow globally, combining 
     

           tradition with modern innovation to keep the sport exciting for future generations.
