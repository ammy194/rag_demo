Football is one of the most popular team sports in the world, played and followed by billions of people across nearly every country. In most of the world, the sport known simply as football is called association football or soccer, and it is governed internationally by FIFA (Fédération Internationale de Football Association).

The modern game of football originated in England in the 19th century when formalized rules were first written down, particularly with the formation of The Football Association in 1863. These rules prohibited carrying the ball and established the basis for the sport as it is known today.

However, games resembling football were played much earlier in history. Evidence shows that ball-kicking games existed in Scotland as early as the 15th century, and similar games appear in Chinese history long before that.

A standard football match is played between two teams of eleven players each, including one goalkeeper on each side. The objective is to score more goals than the opposing team by advancing the ball into the opponent’s goal. Players may use their feet, head, chest, or other parts of the body except their hands or arms to control and pass the ball, with only the goalkeeper allowed to handle the ball within the penalty area.

Matches are typically played on a rectangular grass or artificial turf field, divided into two halves of 45 minutes each, with a 15-minute halftime break. The referee controls the game, enforces the laws, and may issue yellow or red cards for fouls and misconduct.

Football is rich in organized competitions at all levels. The FIFA World Cup, first held in 1930, is the most prestigious international tournament and is watched by huge global audiences. There are also continental championships such as the UEFA European Championship, Copa América, and the Olympic football tournament.

Clubs around the world compete in professional leagues, including the English Premier League, La Liga in Spain, Serie A in Italy, and many others, with rivalries and histories stretching back over a century.

The sport’s rules, known as the Laws of the Game, are maintained by the International Football Association Board (IFAB) and have been in effect, in various forms, since 1863.

Many famous players have left lasting legacies in the sport, becoming icons for their skill, leadership, and influence on the global stage.

Football isn’t just a sport — it’s deeply woven into culture, society, and national identity around the world, uniting people from diverse backgrounds through shared passion and competition.

Matches consist of two halves of 45 minutes, with added stoppage time as decided by the referee.

A goal is scored when the entire ball crosses the goal line between the goalposts and under the crossbar.

Only the goalkeeper may handle the ball, and only inside the penalty area.

Players may use any part of the body except hands or arms to control or pass the ball.

The referee is the authority on the pitch and enforces all laws of the game.

Two assistant referees help the referee, especially with offside and throw-in decisions.

The field of play must be clearly marked and rectangular with fixed minimum and maximum dimensions.

Substitutes are allowed but the number depends on competition rules (often three in professional matches).

The game starts with a kick-off from the centre spot and re-starts this way after each goal.

A ball is out of play when it fully crosses a goal line or touchline, or when play is stopped by the referee.

The offside rule prevents players from gaining unfair advantage by standing near the opponent’s goal when the ball is played.

Fouls and misconduct (e.g., tripping, pushing) are penalised by free kicks or penalty kicks.

Serious fouls or persistent misconduct may earn a yellow card (caution) or red card (sending off).

A direct free kick is awarded for major fouls; a penalty kick if fouls occur inside the penalty area.

Throw-ins are awarded when the ball crosses the touchline; both feet must be on the ground and the ball thrown from behind the head.

A goal kick is awarded when the attacking team last touches the ball before it crosses the defending team’s goal line without scoring.

A corner kick is awarded when the defending team last touches the ball before it crosses their goal line without a goal.
